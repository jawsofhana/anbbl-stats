Match notes
Once again Draconian Devils traveled all the way to Ratten Kameraten. They brought with them an extra apoth (which turned out to be a very wise decision) and a gorgeous Bloodweiser Babe. Before the match it was raining cats and dogs, but luckily it stopped in the same moment the match started. Draconian Devils chose to receive, and what a great start they had; the rats rolled double skulls, re-rolled and ended up with another double skull result; quad skulls !!! In T2 Mathrengyl Bearwalker made a successful quick pass to Dolph. However Rudiger Rattus badly hurted Dr. Riffle, but the extra apoth patched him up instantly. The rats also managed to get the ball loose. In T4 Lothar von Rinnstein grabbed the ball, made a quick pass to Vetle Werfer, who in turn went for it twice, and thought he was in a safe distance from the vicious elves. However Archiball Bait tackled Vetle Werfer with a friendly assist, and once again the ball was loose. In T5 Lothar von Rinnstein picks up the ball, and this time the rat with the ball is guarded by Rattus Norvegicus. Dolph tried to dodge into position, but rolled snake eyes; instant turnover for the rats. In T7 the rats are tempted by a 2D block before scoring, but double skulled again. A RR improved the result slightly to a skull and a push, and Der Rattenführer scored, ending the first half 1-0 in favor of the rats.

The second half started very simular to the first; the rats double skulled, but was saved by yet another RR. Reinhard Rattus killed journeyman, but the poor journeyman seemed to lack his health insurance card, and was denied medical treatment. In T3 the rats double skulled for the fourth time this match, but luckily the RR improved the result to a skull and a push. In the same turn Hunchback the Hunk scored, increasing the rats lead to 2-0. This seemed to anger the crowd, and the elves had a hell of a lot more spectators than the rats, but once again the rats was lucky; 2 elves were beaten up (one of them holding the ball), and just 3 rats were beaten up by the nagry elven mob. In T3 the elves fails a 2+ pass despite using a RR, but the rats failed to get hold of the ball in the following turns. In T5 Dr. Riffle stabbed Torgrim Tyn and inflicted a permanent nerve damage in his healthy leg. The positive side is that Torgrim Tyn is immune to loosing further movement... In the same turn Nemi badly hurted Vetle Werfer, leaving the rats without a thrower. With a GFI Dolph managed to score, reducing the score to 1-2. In T7 Nemi rushes into a fovourable position with the ball, but a lucky blitz from Der Rattenführer nailed her to the ground and got hold of the ball. Michael Maus guarded the brave (and extremely lucky) gutter runner, but with a blitz and 2 GFIs the elves can even the score...Pikkatsju attempted an easy 2+ dodge to assist the planned blitz, but snake eyed the dices,giving the victory to Ratten Kameraten.

This time Rattus Norvegicus worked 15 out of 16 turns, and the single turn he failed to act was not at all critical. This is almost the opposite of his achievement in Draconian Devils - Ratten Kameraten (Season IX, SC2)
