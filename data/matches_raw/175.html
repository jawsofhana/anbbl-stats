Match notes
«Interesting games today, Adolph. The AnBBL-south conference finals was played, and we'll take a look at the bronze-final, between Salacious Sybarites and Elve Elegante. What can you tell us about the game?»

- Well, Cristobal, the High Elves elected to receive and the Dark Elves started to move forward, while their 'sneaky git' who fouled so many rats in Salacious Sybarites - Ratten Kameraten (Season IV, SC Semi Final), was knocked out by a thrown rock amd their best defender knocked himself out on an offencive block.

The Sybarites' running-game got detained in the midfield, but after a while they, had smashed a few elves, including almost killing Fluffy Papaya, who got saved by the team apothecary. Getting a numerical value of homocidical elves made a late touchdown easy pickings, and the Salacious Sybarites lead 1-0 before the second half.

«Yes, but the Elve Elegante was far from beat, with a numerous roster and mucho talent. Though had both teams, and especially their coaches celebrated their league-performance, making the game even more volatile.»

- That's right, Cristobal, they quickly equalized the score, but the Dark Elves had started to indulge in their hatred for their fairer cousins, especially Neriah Downer who by this point in the game had sent two players off pitch. By the end of the game he'd sent three off pitch, two with serious injuries.

This lead the dark elves to dominate the rest of the half, with excessive violence and even jousting Nitnars Phoenixbreaker to the waiting crowd who gave him a niggling injury. They ran a dark-elven 'da cage'-variant up the right flank, were the high-elves were strongest, but I think the emphasis is on *were* strongest.

Uriah Deep, who's the leading running-player of the Salacious Sybarites was eager to score, though, and instead of 'running the clock' he let the Elve Elegantehave a chance to come back, and their star thrower Alatuah Battlepotion, though hindered by his teammates and a row of interceptors, managed to throw a long-bomb to Tellill Grandjewel who caught the ball in-between four! dark elves and then dodged away for an easy touchdown and 2-2!

«Yes, it was a really exciting finish. Plenty of blood and gore and a few cunning plays, both of the running- and the throwing variaty.»

-That's right, Cristobal, but the overtime halfs became a mere formality, especially since the high elves fumbled a catch, was swarmed by the dark elves and after a little tussle, leaving even another high-elf in the injury pile Ezekiel Deceit scored in the half the Elve Elegante was receiving.

In the second overtime half, the high elves just stayed out of trouble, and Michaiah Cunnilinctus ran the ball in for a convincing 4-2 win!

"Who's your MVPs, Adolph?"

- Well, for the Sybarites the ever industrious number 7; Uriah Deep lead the Sybarites like a team captain, and the Elve Elegante's number 2; Asbadd Branchgleam was good at attracting the dangerous Neriah Downer from both Fluffy Papaya, who was dangerously close to bleeding to death after a block and Tellill Grandjewel, who had a great game as a consequence.

"I could have sworn that Neriah Downer brandished a blade, am I right?"

- No, Cristobal, that's illegal and he would have been sent off.
