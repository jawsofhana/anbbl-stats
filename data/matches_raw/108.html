Anarchy Blood Bowl League (AnBBL) offers a way to play and register your table top matches of Blood Bowl in a tournament setting, keep track of player progression and set (and beat) mind blowing (more or less) records!



So, what is Blood Bowl?


AnBBL is Commished by Team Anarchy

Will you win the AnBBL Champions' Trophy?

AnBBL mainly consist of teams originating from two regions of Norway and due to long distances the AnBBL has two conferences. Teams up north attend the North Conference while southern based teams attend the South Conference.

Through the Elite Seasons or the Champions Cup coaches battle to win the ultimate AnBBL Chamipons' Trophy, to get their name on the AnBBL Champions Totem and to become reigning AnBBL Champions!

#If you want to play in the AnBBL, click my picture (on top of our AnBBL Champions Totem) to send an email.

AnBBL are open to all, Fritz von List explains:









"All roads lead to Rome" they say. Well there's also many ways to becoming AnBBL Champion.

The shortest route is to prepare your team through various tournaments and then enter an elite season when they're ready to win! This is also the most common route, so expect to meet some obstacles.

Another option (taking the long road) is to get your team a ticket to the Champions Cup, by winning any AnBBL tournament, and becoming the ultimate Champion when all other teams with a Champions Cup ticket have been knocked out!

- AnBBL Coaces (YOU ALL) must nominate such skills, as explained below.

TEAM CAPTAIN
@ Coaches of ORC and HUMAN teams:
==> nominate your team captains using TEAM BULLETIN with link to player through PID FUNCTION, and edit the PLAYER PROFILE to include a Team Captain note.

Apr. 14th, 2026 - old news
Re-drafting for season3
There's been a lot of changes to team rosters.
- Max 2 positionals on multiple positions that used to be max 4.
- new skill category
- skill access have changed for a lot of players

To take this into account, there is no need to make new teams and loose your franchise!

In stead we can give your team the option of a re-draft :)
- you should buy ReRolls as part of the proces to keep the costs equal to start a new team
- you will keep your hard earned fan base!
- you can opt to re-hire any (legal amount of positional) players - or FIRE them all to start fresh. Maybe this one Fan Favourite (with a minimum ammount of illegal skills for his positional type,) should be allowed to stay on to keep the narratives rollin'?
- you MUST save money to be 11 players minimum after the re-draft and buying fresh players.
- There will be no budget cap, you recieve the funding you've earned through loosing, tie'ing and winning matches!

The AnBBL Staff will make judgement calls on what teams should be forced to re-draft, like:
- High team value Semi Pro XXII teams
- High team value Champions Cup teams
- Teams with an illegal ammount of positionals (i.e. 3 human catchers, 4 gutter runners, etc.)
- Teams with no previous re-draft and a few matches under their belt

Some teams are now in forced re-draft mode.

The re-draft process model is based on season 2 re-draft rules, so the niggling-healing part is at the end, and not up front..!
- The Ref
