Match notes
This game replace the two crossover games between Azgorh Ravens and this game's contestants.

Fluffy Papaya was ill, so Elve Elegante wanted to cancel the game, but two unbelievingly gorgeous Bloodweiser Babes convinced the team to play anyhow. The weather was puring rain, and Ratten Kameraten won the coin toss and (predictibly) chose to receive the ball.

The rats enjoy Blitz Krieg, Rattus Norvegicus starts the offensive, punching a large hole in Elve Elegante`s line (and Tellill Grandjewel, he was out for the game...) Der Rattenführer, Willy Vånd and Manfred von Rinnstein enters deeply into the elves half. Due to the wet conditions Jorge Jordrotte picks up the ball instead of Vetle Werfer. In turn 2 Jorge Jordrotte makes a quick pass to Willy Vånd who in turn easily scores. 1-0. The rats sets up deep, beware of Asbadd Branchgleam. The crowd is angered and throws a rock into the head of goalgetter Willy Vånd. He is K.O.ed and misses the drive. The elves starts a simular offensive to the rats; Tellill Grandjewel and Asbadd Branchgleam enters the half. Tellill Grandjewel ia K.O.ed, and Asbadd Branchgleam is marked by 3 rats. The following turn thrower icone Alatuah Battlepotion attempts a pass, but rolls snake eyes. Reinhard Rattus gets the ball, and in turn 5 he bltzes free and scores. 2-0 to Ratten Kameraten. Once again the crowd throws a rock, but this time Ihlaon Throwmaster is his, leaving him stunned. In turn 6 Asbadd Branchgleam makes a quick pass to Sprintbird George, reducing the score to 1-2. The rats are angered, and Rattus Norvegicus blitzes. This time he is a bit to eager and double skulls. Being a loner means that the re roll spent is spent for nothing. Late in the half Asbadd Branchgleam had a 5+ oppurtunity to pick up the ball en the rats end xone, but he fails. 4 elves are out of the game, and no rats.

In the second half Jabo Schreck badly hurts Hannidd Wiseblow. Alatuah Battlepotion is nailed by Rattus Norvegicus, but the rats fail to notice the shy, little elf Targipl Ironseer. Alatuah Battlepotion makes a perfect pass to him, but Targipl Ironseer fails to catch the wet ball. Der Rattenführer gets hold of the ball instead, and makes a hand over to the ace Willy Vånd. He in turn makes a short pass to Manfred von Rinnstein, but also him fails to catch the slippery ball. Even Ihlaon Throwmaster slips the ball a moment after. In turn 3 Manfred von Rinnstein makes a T.D. He was blitzed free by Willy Vånd. A riot erupts, and the raf ads an extra turn to the game. Alatuah Battlepotion dashes through, and even to three dice attempts to knock him over fails for Rattus Norvegicus. Alatuah Battlepotion is, however, sourrounded by 6 rats, but instead of breaking loose he throws a short pass to Targipl Ironseer. He has to go for it twice, but still scores. 2-3. The weather suddenly changes to very sunny conditions instead. Vetle Werfer makes a short pass to Manfred von Rinnstein, but this time Hihahneas Holytackle intercepts the ball. He dodges loose and makes a short pass to Asbadd Branchgleam. In turn 6 Asbadd Branchgleam scores another T.D., equalling the score to 3-3. This is quite an achievment, since it was made with just 8 elves against 11 healthy rats. Then they set up Asbadd Branchgleam is a bit to close to the side zone. Hannibal Hector can´t resist the oppurtunity to get rid of the annoying player, so he is crowd surfed. With only their superior throwers left Elve Elegante fails to stop the rats offensive, so Ratten Kameraten win the game 4-3.

MVP for Ratten Kameraten was Rattus Norvegicus, not strange since he started the game with a casualty in the first turn, and for stopping the highly effective elven catchers time and time again.

MVP for Elve Elegante was Urnaac Pantherstealer, the reason for this is unknown.
