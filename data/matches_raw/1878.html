Match notes
Onsdags Bowl in Wrathalla 02.08.23

Black Gulf Buccaneers vs. Dark Moon Wranglers

The Black Gulf Buccaneers and the Dark Moon Wranglers squared off for the first time this season, in what started out as a very sunny day.
4000 dark elves and 3000 goblin fans had turned up to catch some sweet Blood Bowl action in the very bright sunlight.
The Buccaneers won the coin toss before the match, and decided to play defence in the first drive of the game.
Just as the first kick of the game happened, the sun disappeared and the heaven above opened up and unleashed pouring rain onto the pitch, making it harder to handle the ball.

The game opened with a lot of violence as the trolls, Troll Wayne and Morgan Pain started blocking and clearing the line of scrimmage, while Dick Sawyer, the Looney with a chainsaw went after one of the linemen. Luckily for the Buccaneers they withstood the opening acts of violence with nothing worse than a few knockdowns and stunned linelves. Raelyn Darksting, one of the dark elves blitzers quickly recognized the threat of the Looney, and charged him immediately, and as the chainsaw is as dangerous to the looney wielding it as it is to everyone else, Dick Sawyer`s time on the pitch was over.
He suffered a niggling injury as Miss Darksting smashed him to the ground with the chainsaw flailing wildly about in his puny grip.
Moments later Troll Wayne krumped his way onwards as he blitzed Malekith Hackblade in the middle of the pich, sending him off on a stretcher, looking badly hurt.
Miss Darksting`s bloodlust was not sated with the spilling of looney blood, and went after another goblin, this time it was Sock Hollyday that got badly hurt and had to be carried off the pitch.

After the very violent first 2 rounds of the first half, the game slowed down a bit as the Wranglers carefully moved the ball upwards the pitch on the left flank, and the Buccaneers tried a patient defence to see it the goblins would make a mistake they could take advantage of.
In round 7 this moment came, but Wild Gim Highkok made no mistake as the stunty fellow dodged his way straight through 4 or 5 defenders and rushing in the first touchdown of the game!
A touchdown in round 7 meant that the Buccaneers had a slight chance to equalize before the half-time whistle, but they squandered it as their attempt ended up being too weak with not enough support for Illmarion Fellheart, as he rushed towards the endzone almost on his own.
The Wranglers managed to gang up on him and wrangle him to the ground foiling his attempt.

1-0 lead to the Wranglers at half-time, as Dynamite Dan got sent off for using bombs and almost blowing himself and a few others up just before the half-time whistle.

The second half had another violent opening, as the Wranglers opened with a blitz-defence maneuverer. Rip Swinger and his wrecking ball had entered the pitch for the second half, and he was raring to go as he was unleashed and pointed towards the Buccaneers offensive lineup. Wreaking havoc as he pummelled at least 3 dark elves to the ground before the rest of the goblins repositioned themselves slightly to further monkey-wrench the Buccaneers obvious plan of attack.
The Buccaneers original plan got scrapped as soon as the ball landed, as they now had a fanatic spinning around with a wrecking ball in the midst of their ranks. Their main focus now was to remove the fanatic and regroup on the opposite flank where the ball had landed and see if they could muster a new offence.
They managed to get enough dark elves into support to overpower the fungus powered fanatic (with a re-roll) and remove him from play, and reposition slightly towards the other flank, but as the runner went to collect the ball, the pouring rain and wet circumstances proved more challenging for the Buccaneers than the goblins, and the ball was loose and somewhat open at the left flank as the Wranglers went about with a counter-attack play.
The play involved flinging a goblin towards the ball with uncanny precision and although he did not stick the landing, he got up and dodged his way through 2 dark elves, collecting the wet ball, before dodging towards safer ground.
The ground wasn`t much safer though as the Buccaneers no longer had to worry about the fanatic, they all closed in on the cheeky goblin with the ball and managed to blitz the ball free before long.
The Wranglers started to run into problems as they slowly began to run out of goblins on the pitch, with a few out with injuries, a few more sent off, and a few knocked out, and now the Buccaneers finally had managed to get their hands on the slippery ball and secured it in a cage formation in the middle of the pitch.
As the Buccaneers finally made a break towards the endzone with one of their blitzers carrying the ball, Morgan Pain threw another goblin after him, hitting him right across the shoulders smashing him to the ground and the ball came loose once again. The blitzer only got his pride hurt, but the poor goblin used as a projectile got knocked out as he plunged into the spiky dark elf armour.
This only slowed the Buccaneers down, as they had more players around the ball and Raelynn Darksting looked poised to gain control of it.

Troll Wayne was supposed to throw another goblin into the fray in another attempt to thwart the Buccaneers attack, but instead he opted to make Farthur Gnorkman a tasty snack, as all the blocking and throwing goblins had made him really hungry at this point.

Miss Darksting wasted no time as Troll Wayne gobbled down on the misfortunate goblin and rushed in for the equalising touchdown.
1-1 in an even contest, with plenty of time left for either team to win it.

The Wranglers now even more outnumbered after Troll Wayne`s mid-game snack, had to try to muster one more attacking play, but the Buccaneers managed a superb kick, as the ball ended up all the way in the corner of the endzone on the left flank.
The Wranglers managed to collect the ball, but as their team was stretched thin, the Buccaneers sprinted through the gaps and put pressure on the goblins as the final whistle drew closer.
The goblins couldn`t handle the pressure, as the ball carrier got tackled to the ground when he failed a dodge attempt, and the ball came loose. The Buccaneers where gifted a last minute scoring opportunity, and Mallus Spiteblade did not squander it, as he got his hands on the ball and sprinted in the winning touchdown in the Buccaneers last turn of the game!

A great game of Blood Bowl in the pouring rain, with the final score a 2-1 win to the Black Gulf Buccaneers, and 3-1 in the casualty box.
