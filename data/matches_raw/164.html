Match notes
«Well, we're off. The teami(Azg) has elected to receive in front of a equal-sized crowd of 26'000, and the Goblins have put up a strong defence. Well, strong as stunties can make it anyway, right Jonny?»

Right, Skip. It seems that the Poshtown Pursesnatchers have set up to take advantage of the Ravens' slow, or rather varied movement.

«Well, the Ravens move upwards the right, reshuffling their offence. They push forward, but burst right through at first attempt.»

That's right, and here is Nobbla Blackwart and a running chainsaw... ouch! there goes the leading Wight down, and Bomber Dribblesnot readies a bomb!

«He throws it, and it explodes in a hustle of bodies, taking down five bodies, including two of his teammates!»

Well, the commotion caused the ball to get loose, and it's picked up by the ghoul Winston, who runs into Nobbla's running chainsaw and is stunned!

«Only a few goblins is on their feet, but the Azgorh Ravens have only a few zombies left standing, and they're all tying up the Pursesnatchers' Trolls. Fart Intestines picks up the ball and runs right field... and runs, noone can stop him it seems.»

Well, there's the werewolf on pitch smelling the air, and he dodges past the goblin charged with marking him... and I can't believe it, the little runt tackles him!

«Yeah, the slow moving Ravens seem unable to catch Fart Intestines, let alone break free from the Pursesnatchers' line.They seem to vent their frustration on the downed troll Vomit Bellyheaver, and they embark on a foul galore spree!»

But they're really not co-ordinated enough, and Fart Intestines sure takes his time celebrating before running over the scoring line, taunting the home fans, in classic unsportsmanslike goblin fashion. You'll see that he runs off with the ball as well.

<<< ----- >>>

«Welcome, folks, to the second half between Azgorh Ravens and Poshtown Pursesnatchers starts with the goblins in a surprise lead, 1-0, and they will also start to receive. What can we expect in the second half, Jonny?»

No time, we're off, and the Pursesnatchers are fueld by their first half performance and start a fraction earlier than the Ravens expected. The ball lands deep.

«Right, Bomber Dribblesnot is still on-pitch, and the referee sports a brand new gold-watch to tell the time.

... Not that we insunuates that there's a connection, right Jonny?»

«Yes, but I can already see that the Necromancer-coach Heinrich Vom Doom starts to work his unholy ork-magic... it seems like he will still... eh... 'live' still further this season.»

Right, not a great start by the Azgorh Ravens' defence, and the goblins form a protective cage around their team captain; Groo Sneakers, who quickly dash through as he sees and opening.

«OMGosh, the Goblins lead 2-0 over the team that recently crushed Triple Pox Brigade in the Triple Pox Brigade - Azgorh Ravens (Season IV, Semi Pro) game. What's going through the mind of Heinrich Von Doom, twin-brother too Heimlich Von Doom, coach to the Alpha-league team; Iron Rock 69'ers. What do you say, Jonny?»

First of all, the Goblins have had a great season, and we must not forget that da Sneaky Runtz won this Season's Keffo Cup. The goblins aren't so bad, and they have a grave tendency to bring... eh, ability-equalizingequipment to their matches. It's a wonder why the referees aren't more aware.

«Well, the 2-0 touchdown was scored against only five Ravens, four zombies and a flesh golem. Now the Ravens field eight players against the Pursesnatchers' eleven. The kick is hiiiigh...»

The kick is shallow in the Ravens left-back field and a lone zombie scramble for it. It seems that the referee is substituted, though. Eh, a zombie dressed in stripes?!???
