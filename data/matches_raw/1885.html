Match notes
Onsdagsball @ The Joker

Real Horrorshow BBC vs. Emperor’s Wolves

A glorious night of Blood Bowl when 2 teams made their league debut, as The Emperor’s Wolves took on Real Horrorshow BBC.

Real kicked off the game in the first drive, and the Wolves wasted no time as they got off to a great start. The undead on the other hand, looked rather rusty as they botched most of what they attempted in the opening rounds of the game, making it an easy job for Gilon Misug, the human Blitzer no. 8 to score the first touchdown in round 3.

The undead lived up to their name as they continued to put on a real horrorshow of a performance in the next drive, as they fumbled the ball and most other things. By round 5 Gilon Misug once again found himself on the scoresheet as he rushed in 2-0 for the Wolves and the undead team looked to be in shambles.

The Head Necromancer for Real Horrorshow BBC opted for a tactical substitution as he changed dice before the next kick-off.

This proved to be a good move as the undead finally started to perform, and by the time the half-time whistle sounded, they had managed to put one blitzer and one lineman in the casualty box, and one lineman in the K.O booth, as well as having Lord Liverbelch score a touchdown of their own.

In the second half Real Horrorshow carried on performing well, but the Emperor’s Wolves looked demoralized as they found themselves slightly outnumbered as Real were on the attack once again.
The undead looked certain to score early on, but the Head Necromancer opted to stall the game in an effort to try and remove more humans from the pitch before going into a new drive.
This backfired as they nearly squandered the scoring opportunity as well as losing the brawl, as they suffered a K.O on Lord Liverbelch, the ball carrier, and Carlin the skeleton got himself badly hurt but got better quickly as he regenerated.
Real managed to salvage the situation before it got any worse though, and scored the equalizer in round 13 when Ra’s Al the Ghoul Runner got on the scoresheet.

In the next rounds the Wolves were very unfortunate as the ball landed all the way in the corner of the endzone at the kick-off, and as Steven Willard the human thrower took his time when he went to pick up the ball, he fumbled with his sure hands, dropping the ball right next to the endzone. In the meantime, Wight Eisenhower had moved into position in anticipation of such an event, and now found himself in a perfect position to blitz the thrower stood in the corner of the pitch.
And so he did, the thrower got plowed into the crowd, before the wight picked up the loose ball and now found himself all alone at the endzone with no one around to stop him scoring in the final round of the game.

3-2 win for Real Horrorshow BBC as they made a solid comeback after a terrible start to the game.
