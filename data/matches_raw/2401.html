Match notes
After the humiliation against the Skrappaz Orcs, the men of Varhall returned home emotionally broken, spiritually hollow, and still faintly dripping motor oil into the parking lot outside Nellie’s Bar, the sacred cultural center of the league, where many difficult world problems were solved every night.

Coach ColdNChilly entered first, ordered twelve Bloodweissers and one black coffee “for tactical purposes,” and called the undead coach shortly before midnight.

“Vil dø slæss litt meir?”

The answer came immediately.

“Absolutely.”

And so the rematch was born.

The two teams spent the evening drinking enough Bloodweisser to legally qualify as industrial solvents, until eventually someone suggested settling their frustrations on the pitch instead of in the parking lot with tire irons.

This was widely considered mature.

The undead coach, still glowing with pride after crippling Gær-Hærm “96” in the previous match, arrived eager to recreate the moment. He prowled the sideline with the smug expression of a man who had discovered a reliable method for ruining another human being’s week.

Unfortunately for him, the Khorne boys from Solør had spent their time bottling their shame into a dangerous new substance: Motivation.

The opening drive began with the undead line shambling forward like a tax office after a gas leak. But this time Varhall met them head-on.

The kick sailed deep.

Æljen and Grillkongen exploded downfield immediately, forming what local commentators later described as “Solør-Elgen Expressen” a horrifying wall of horns, sweat, and anger. The undead ball carrier suddenly found himself isolated in the backfield while the Khorne players closed in like garage mechanics around an unpaid invoice.

The first real explosion came when Benny Blodøks met Michael Jackson near midfield.

Nobody was entirely sure what Benny hit him with. Technically it was a block, but Michael Jackson’s face detached completely in a shower of wrappings and old dust, spiraling into the crowd like a thrown dinner plate.

For several glorious seconds, Benny stood frozen in triumph while the Varhall supporters screamed themselves hoarse.

Then, to everyone’s irritation, Michael Jackson regenerated moments later and was spotted sitting in the dugout with his nose upside down.

It did not stop the undead from taking vengeace later, though, when Tjetu Makhai made minced meat out of Kevin "Kjøttdeig". The apothecary wrapped him back up, like a taco. nobody noticed the difference. Although several onlookers became unexplainably hungry.

The greatest surprise was maybe the matter of Mr. Nobody.

Nobody quite knew where he had come from.

One day he simply appeared outside the stadium wearing a stained leather jacket, carrying a duffel bag that smelled faintly of diesel and boiled sausage. The coach asked if he could play Blood Bowl.

Mr. Nobody reportedly shrugged and answered:

“Litt.”

That was good enough for Varhall. He was just going to die on the line anyway.

Officially he was only a journeyman replacement, dragged in to fill the gaps left by injuries, concussions, and what the team doctor described as “eager enthusiasm.” The supporters barely noticed him during warmups. He looked utterly ordinary beside the screaming lunatics and mutant horrors that made up the rest of the squad.

Then the match started.

From the opening drive, Mr. Nobody played with the strange calm of a man who had absolutely nothing left to lose. While everyone else hurled themselves into violence like battering rams, he simply wandered into the right places at exactly the right moments.

When Æljen and Grillkongen cracked open the undead formation early in the first half, it was Mr. Nobody who slipped quietly through the gap unnoticed. The undead defense was so busy trying to survive the frenzy around midfield that nobody realized he even had the ball until he was already halfway down the pitch.

Then, with all the urgency of a man heading toward the last gas-station hotdog before closing time, Mr. Nobody crossed the line for Varhall’s opening touchdown.

The crowd exploded.

Not because the touchdown itself was especially impressive by Blood Bowl standards, but because every single supporter in the stadium immediately became convinced that Mr. Nobody was “one of ours.”

Nobody knew his real name.

Nobody knew where he lived.

One old supporter swore he was Kjell-Arne from Flisa who disappeared in 2019 after a disagreement involving a snowmobile and municipal property. Another insisted he was a former truck driver banned from Sweden 'cause of Snus-traficking. A third claimed he had emerged fully grown from the Glomma River during a thunderstorm.

And when the dust finally settled after the 3 - 1 slaughter, it was Mr. Nobody the crowd chose as MVP.

The announcement seemed to surprise him more than anyone else.

After the match a reporter asked him what his passions are in life, and he answered: “Snus i gommen og Hemmbrent i springen.”. The coach, who had planned to hire a Bloodseeker, scratched out the other name and hired him on the spot.
