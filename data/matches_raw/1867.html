Match notes
Perfect weather for Blood Bowl this evening as 5000 frenzied Khorne fanatics and 4000 greenskins turned up to witness the spectacle, as the Kleive Cleavers took on Da Rag-Tag Snatchaz in what was expected to be a gore drenched battle on the pitch.

Da Snatchas won the coin toss before the game and decided to defend in the first drive.
The game opened with plenty of blocking actions back and forth without any serious consequences, as the Cleavers gathered the ball and pushed on up through on the right flank.

In round 4 the Cleavers managed their first casualty of the game, as Marauder Lineman no.2, Skaraclash Spinebreaker took down Black Orc no. 8, Relch Da Stained (underpants?) causing a serious injury and forcing him to miss the rest of this match and the next.

In round 5, Marauder Lineman no.9, Khurgar Fleshtearer got sent off for fouling and knocking out Zulgash, the Black Orc wearing no. 10, as the Cleavers where through to the endzone, destined to score the first touchdown after having smashed their way through Da Rag-Tag Snatchaz’s defence and filling up the KO booth as well as sending a Goblin flying off the pitch and into the reserve box.
In round 5 the Cleavers’ no. 10 Skarri Skullgrinder, their only Khorngor strutted into the endzone for his first touchdown of the season.

Round 7 opened with a bang, as Quank the Troll smashed Khorar Bladerender with a mighty blow to the face, causing the lineman to spend the rest of the match in the infirmary to recover from some real bad hurting.

Round 8 saw Bloodseeker no. 4, Khârnox Skullclaimer, all riled up from witnessing the carnage, unleash his wrath upon Huro-Huro the goblin no. 5, and smashing him with such ferocity that the poor goblin will miss the next match as well as the rest of this.

At half time the Cleavers looked pretty comfortable, as they were in the lead and seemed to have the upper hand in the fights on the pitch, and several of the Black Orcs had been KO’ed hard enough that they needed some more time to recover.
As the second half got under way the Cleavers where outnumbering Da Snatchaz slightly and should be in control.

In round 10, bad became worse for Da Snatchaz as Harrk Devilgrin, Black Orc no. 7 opted to go for a “both down” against Angroz The Red Fury, the Cleavers’ mighty Bloodspawn. Instead of just pushing him around a bit, Harrk Devilgrin felt lucky and confident on breaking the bloodspawn’s armour.
He failed to hurt the bloodspawn however, but The Red Fury’s claw took Harrk Devilgrin’s head off, making him far too dead to play Blood Bowl anymore.

In round 12 the Cleavers, and Skarri Skullgrinder found their way into the endzone with the ball once more, making it 2-0. But the fighting was still the main attraction of this game as both teams had less and less players on the pitch.
In round 16 Da Ra-Tag Snatchaz had a glorious opportunity to score a consolation goal, as goblin no. 12, Eelberd dodged trice to get through to the endzone while carrying the ball, but as he rushed to score, he fell on his face and wasted his chance for glory.

Final score 2-0 for the Cleavers, and 3-1 in casualties.
