Feb. 18th, 2008 - old news
Top of the group
Triple Pox Brigade have had a brilliant start so far in the season, winning over both Duftham Skulltakers and Iron Rock 69'ers, and only losing against Kadrin Kazad Nutcrackers.

The team has multi purpose characters like Rabs Dawg, and the agile and lighting fast rotter(!) Burt Badd. The later is by the way the first and only rotter ever to enter the Hall of Fame for "Most experienced Players", with a total of 38 SPPs so far in his career.
- Hannibal Hector
 
 
Feb. 10th, 2008 - old news
Promising start
Back in Season II Triple Pox Brigade had a 100% win rate (but only two games played...)

After a long pause, and a new coach they dropped to a miserable 11.1% win rate in Season IV (one win, 9 games...)

Season V have started quite well; a 50% win rate, and the only loss so far was just bad luck against Kadrin Kazad Nutcrackers. Hannibal Hectors bad memory played a part in the loss too; he forgot completely about the bucket of grease he had brought with him "just in case". With greased shoes KraKrac Kurnuts probably never would have made the necessary go for it in the final turn in the second half, and the game would have gone into overtime instead. And with players like Koff Pox, an armor value of 10 on several dwarves doesn´t help at all...
- Hannibal Hector
 
 
Nov. 11th, 2007 - old news
Fall of Nurgle
Triple Pox Brigade seemed to have found the winning formula, but the last match against Azgorh Ravens was a major setback.

The team only has two pestigors, and both were taken out of the game relatively early. This was critical for the teams ball handling capability, and shows that the team should invest in more pestigors as soon as possible.

The bashing players of the team are specialized in smacking up almost any race and position, and they did their job well, but this time their hard work didn`t seem to pay off; the stubborn necromantic players simply refused to leave the pitch. Only fouling and crowd surfing seemed to work, until Big Poppa Pox inflicted the teams first "real" casuality. It was a shame that this happened in the eight turn in the second half.

Anyhow Azgorh Ravens won a deserved 3-0 victory. Hannibal Hector was afraid Big Poppa Pox would hug him after the game, but he was relieved then he discovered that Big Poppa Pox instead gave him a gift for further inspiration.

The gift seems to be perfect for an academic rat fond of all types of puzzles; he was given a black lacquered puzzle box with inlays of pure gold...
- Hannibal Hector
